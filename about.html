About KayaqAI

An AI buying assistant that helps Indians choose electronics with confidence.

What we do

KayaqAI is a research-first electronics comparison platform for the Indian market. We combine product specs, real-time prices across key retailers, aggregated user reviews and crowdsourced opinions into a single AI-assisted recommendation experience. Users can ask our AI assistant what they need, run a guided requirement wizard, or browse and filter by category.

Our approach

We do not sell products and we do not run paid ranking. Recommendations are weighted on hardware capability, software longevity, aggregated review sentiment, and how closely a product matches what the user has told us. When a user clicks through to a retailer, we may earn an affiliate commission, but this never influences ranking.

  • Air Conditioners
  • Washing Machines
  • Refrigerators

More categories are added as our catalogue and review base expands.
